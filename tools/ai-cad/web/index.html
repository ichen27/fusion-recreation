Supported geometry

Blocks, convex and simple concave XY profile extrusions, and circular or tapered round stock. Tapered stock has two positive, differing radii and supports placement, measurement and export. Separate round holes can pass through the stock or stop at one shared flat-bottom depth. All supported solids can move, quarter-turn and form linked copies. Planar solids support chained cuts by arbitrary planes. Planar Booleans join, subtract or intersect solids, including cavities and separate pieces. Exact affine relationships preserve declared dimensions. Curved intersections, fillets and a general sketch constraint solver remain unsupported. Preview validates each edit.

Draw a planar profile

3–16 points in one simple loop. Inward corners are allowed; no crossing or collinear corners. Preview validates the complete model before anything is saved.

Through-holes

Through holes span the stock. Blind holes start at the top and leave a positive flat floor. Counterbores widen the upper section of a through-hole for a fastener head. A group shares recess depth and recess rim radius. Countersinks use a conical recess; its angle follows the chosen radius and depth. Circles must stay inside the outline and apart. Existing hole IDs survive edits. Remove all holes to remove the group. Unused parameters are retained. Preview checks before saving.

Hollow solid

For a top opening use (0, 0, 1). Reverse a sign for the opposite face. Uncheck to keep a closed cavity.

Convex planar stock only. Wall thickness is perpendicular to each retained face. Curved and unsupported irrational offsets reject.

Plane cut

For example, normal (1, 0, 1) and offset 40 keeps x + z ≤ 40. A cut cannot remove the entire solid. Previous Boolean results may have cavities, holes or separate pieces. Simple concave sketch stock is supported; analytic curved stock and round hole groups are unsupported.

Combine planar solids

Start with unperforated planar solids or previous Boolean results. Concave sketches, cavities, through-holes and separate pieces are supported. Analytic curved inputs, edge/point-only joins and empty results reject.

Keep common volume

Convex planar solids only. Circular, perforated and concave shapes are unsupported. Touching faces without shared volume cannot form a solid.
