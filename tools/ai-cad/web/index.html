Supported geometry

Blocks, convex and simple concave XY profile extrusions, and circular stock. Separate round holes can pass through the stock or stop at one shared flat-bottom depth. Convex planar solids support chained cuts by arbitrary planes. Exact affine relationships preserve declared dimensions. Curved intersections, fillets and a general sketch constraint solver remain unsupported. Preview validates each edit.

Draw a planar profile

3–16 points in one simple loop. Inward corners are allowed; no crossing or collinear corners. Preview validates the complete model before anything is saved.

Through-holes

Through holes span the stock. Blind holes start at the top and leave a positive flat floor. All holes in a group share one depth. Circles must stay inside the outline and apart. Existing hole IDs survive edits. Remove all holes to remove the group. Unused parameters are retained. Preview checks before saving.

Plane cut

For example, normal (1, 0, 1) and offset 40 keeps x + z ≤ 40. A cut cannot remove the entire solid. Preview before saving.
